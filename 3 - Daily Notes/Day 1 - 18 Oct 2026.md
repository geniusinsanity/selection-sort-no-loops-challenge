## Entry 1: Can booleans alone make a loop?

- **Time:** __:__
- **Tool / medium:** Claude (AI chat)
- **Place / source:** AI answer, no outside source
- **Goal:** Question 1
- **What I asked:** Can a loop be built from booleans only?
- **Result:** No. Boolean logic (AND, OR, NOT) is a pure function: inputs go in, one answer comes out. Repetition needs something extra: memory with feedback (a latch plus a clock), unrolling (fixed size only), an outside stepper (cellular automata such as Rule 110), or self-application (lambda calculus, which is recursion in disguise).
- **Right or wrong?:** Right as theory, but it does not solve the assignment yet.
- **Mistake (if any):** I was looking for a loop made only of booleans. The hint means booleans make the decisions, not the repetition.
- **What I learned:** A loop needs time, memory, or self-reference. Booleans give decisions only.
- **Next step:** Find what can supply the repetition when loops are forbidden.
